Privacy Architecture

Your knowledge stays yours. Always.

Haarks Clinic is built on a per-surgeon isolation architecture. No surgeon's knowledge is ever accessible to another. Privacy is not a feature; it is the foundation.

AI Isolation

Your Haarks AI runs in a private namespace. No other surgeon can access your data, ever.

You Own the Knowledge

Every output, every indexed document, every AI response belongs to you. We own the infrastructure. You own the intelligence. You can delete everything, at any time.

AES-256 + TLS 1.3

All data is encrypted at rest with AES-256 and in transit with TLS 1.3. Industry-standard encryption with no exceptions, not configurable, not optional.

Zero Shared Learning

Your knowledge never trains shared AI models. No aggregation, no cross-surgeon learning, no data used to improve any product other than your own private layer.

Your Files Aren't Retained

Files are sent over encrypted transport (TLS) for transient, in-memory server-side processing, then discarded. Haarks does not store or retain your source files; your original files stay in your own cloud drive. To make your knowledge searchable, we retain derived search data, such as extracted text, metadata, and embeddings, in a private layer isolated to your account.
