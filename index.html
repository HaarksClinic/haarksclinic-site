The Problem

Surgeons accumulate knowledge.
They rarely get to use it.

A decade of surgical practice generates an enormous body of personal knowledge. But it's locked away in formats that can't be retrieved at the moment you need them most.

01 — Fragmentation

Knowledge is everywhere. Accessible nowhere.

Surgical notes in Google Drive. Learnings recorded on voice memos. Research papers in a Dropbox folder nobody opens. Presentations from conferences two years ago. It all exists — but none of it is connected.

02 — Recall

The case in front of you needs the case behind you.

A surgeon's greatest resource is their own pattern of experience. But memory under pressure is unreliable. The technique that worked brilliantly last year, the complication you learned from — it should surface automatically, not after a frantic search.

03 — Legacy

Years of clinical learning shouldn't retire with you.

The knowledge built over a career is irreplaceable. Today it lives only in formats tied to specific apps and devices. There is no structured way to compound it, organise it, or carry it forward as a living, growing professional asset.

Privacy Architecture

Your knowledge stays yours. Always.

Haarks Clinic is built on a zero-isolation architecture. No surgeon's knowledge is ever accessible to another. Privacy is not a feature — it is the foundation.

Private Namespace Isolation

Your knowledge runs in a private, isolated namespace. No other surgeon's query can reach your data — enforced at the infrastructure level, not just the application layer.

You Own the Knowledge

Every output, every indexed document, every AI response belongs to you. We own the infrastructure. You own the intelligence. You can delete everything, at any time.

AES-256 + TLS 1.3

All data is encrypted at rest with AES-256 and in transit with TLS 1.3. Industry-standard encryption with no exceptions — not configurable, not optional.

Zero Shared Learning

Your knowledge never trains shared AI models. No aggregation, no cross-surgeon learning, no data used to improve any product other than your own private layer.

Files Stay on Your Storage

Documents are indexed from your Google Drive and OneDrive — they are never copied to Haarks servers. The intelligence is extracted. The file remains exactly where it is.
