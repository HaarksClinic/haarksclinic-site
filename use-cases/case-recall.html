Case recall.

Ask what you did in your own previous cases, and see the records behind the answer. Your experience, retrievable in seconds instead of scattered across memory and notes.

Get Haarks Clinic

The problem

You have operated on thousands of cases, but that experience lives in your memory, in operative notes, and in files you rarely reopen. When a familiar case appears, recalling exactly what you did last time, and why, is slow and imprecise.

How Haarks approaches it

Every case you log is structured and connected into your private knowledge layer. When you ask a question, Haarks retrieves the relevant cases you documented and answers from them, showing the sources so you can verify. It is retrieval of your own record, not generic advice.

What the surgeon asks

  • "What did I do in my last four valgus TKRs?"
  • "Show me my previous cases involving a difficult airway."
  • "What complications did I document in my previous bowel resections?"

What can be retrieved

  • Your documented approach, findings, and technique for matching cases.
  • Implants, materials, and constructs you recorded.
  • Complications and follow-up you logged, with links to the source cases.

Limitations

Case recall reflects only what you have documented, and only for your own account. Haarks does not diagnose, recommend treatment, or make clinical decisions, and is not a medical device. All clinical decisions remain the responsibility of the treating surgeon.

Who it is for

Any surgeon who logs cases and wants their own history to be searchable, across every specialty.
