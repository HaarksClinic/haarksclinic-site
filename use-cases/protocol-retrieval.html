Protocol retrieval.

Find the protocol, technique, or guideline you have saved, in your own words, without digging through folders and drives.

Get Haarks Clinic

The problem

Protocols, checklists, and techniques accumulate across documents, presentations, and notes. Finding the right one at the right moment means remembering where you put it, and what you called it.

How Haarks approaches it

You connect your own documents from your storage into your Knowledge Vault. Haarks indexes the derived text so you can retrieve the right protocol by asking in natural language, and it shows which document the answer came from. Your original files stay in your connected drive.

What the surgeon asks

  • "Which protocol did I use for post-op anticoagulation after arthroplasty?"
  • "Show me my saved technique note for a difficult laparoscopic dissection."
  • "What is in my enhanced-recovery checklist?"

What can be retrieved

  • The relevant passage from protocols and documents you have connected.
  • A link back to the source document so you can open the original.
  • Your own notes and techniques, surfaced alongside related cases.

Limitations

Retrieval reflects only the documents you have connected. A protocol being retrievable does not make it current or correct, that remains your judgment. Haarks is not a clinical decision-support system or medical device. All clinical decisions remain the responsibility of the treating surgeon.

Who it is for

Surgeons who maintain their own protocols and reference material and want it searchable in one private place.
