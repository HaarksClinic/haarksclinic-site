Regulatory Developments

FDA finalises guidance on change-control plans for AI-enabled devices

What happened

The FDA issued final guidance on marketing-submission recommendations for a Predetermined Change Control Plan (PCCP) for AI-enabled device software functions, describing how planned model changes can be pre-specified, validated, and implemented.

Why it matters

It may affect how AI-enabled medical technologies are evaluated and updated after they reach the market.

Source: U.S. FDA ↗Dec 2024
Medical AI

FDA updates its public list of AI/ML-enabled medical devices

What happened

The FDA maintains and periodically updates a public, downloadable list of AI/ML-enabled medical devices authorised for marketing in the United States.

Why it matters

A practical reference for understanding which categories of AI-enabled devices have reached the US market.

Source: U.S. FDA ↗Updated 2024
Medical AI

WHO publishes ethics guidance for large multi-modal AI models in health

What happened

The World Health Organization released guidance on the ethics and governance of large multi-modal models (LMMs) in health, with recommendations across the AI lifecycle for governments, developers, and providers.

Why it matters

It sets out widely-referenced principles for responsible use of generative AI in health settings.

Source: WHO ↗Jan 2024
Surgical Robotics

Intuitive receives FDA clearance for the da Vinci 5 robotic system

What happened

Intuitive announced FDA 510(k) clearance for da Vinci 5, its next-generation multiport robotic system, later reporting clearance for certain cardiac procedures.

Why it matters

Signals continued iteration in the multiport surgical-robotics platforms available to surgical teams.

Source: Intuitive ↗Mar 2024
Surgical Robotics

Medtronic's Hugo robotic-assisted surgery system cleared for urology

What happened

Medtronic announced FDA clearance of its Hugo robotic-assisted surgery (RAS) system for urologic procedures, following its Expand URO clinical study.

Why it matters

Adds another robotic-assisted option in the soft-tissue surgical-robotics market.

Source: Medtronic ↗Dec 2025
Surgical Technology

Stryker expands its Mako robotics portfolio with handheld robotics

What happened

Stryker announced an expansion of its Mako robotics portfolio, introducing a handheld robotics system for total knee replacement alongside its robotic-arm platform.

Why it matters

Reflects diversification of orthopaedic robotic-assistance formats available to surgeons.

Source: Stryker ↗2026
