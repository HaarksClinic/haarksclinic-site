About Haarks

Surgery has never been short of knowledge.It has been short of memory.

Haarks is building private intelligence for surgery — a way for a surgeon's own cases, protocols and sources to become something they can reach, not something they lose.

A career is an act of accumulation

Every surgeon builds a private body of knowledge, case by case, over a lifetime.

  • A case becomes a lesson.
  • A complication becomes experience.
  • A protocol becomes instinct.
  • A paper changes a practice.
  • An implant becomes familiar.
  • A decision becomes part of how you think.

And then most of it scatters, across drives, folders, notes, images, videos and memory. The knowledge survives. The ability to reach it, at the moment it would help, often does not.

Most medical AI begins with the world and asks: what can I tell you?
Haarks begins with the surgeon and asks: what do you already know?

The difference is easy to state and large in practice. One answers from everything, in general. The other answers from your own work, in particular, and shows you where each answer came from.

Why Haarks exists

Haarks is a private AI for surgery. It answers from your own cases and protocols, with sources.

Not a voice over your shoulder in theatre. Not a second opinion on a patient. A way to ask your own experience a question, and get your own experience back, organised and cited. The judgment stays where it belongs: with the surgeon.

On privacy: files are sent over encrypted transport (TLS) for transient, in-memory server-side processing, then discarded. Haarks does not store or retain your source files. Your original files stay in your own cloud drive.

From memory to intelligence

A career in surgery should not end as an archive, a collection of files no one reopens.

Every case can join a growing body of knowledge. Every protocol can become easier to find. Every documented complication can become easier to recall. Every source can stay attached to its answer. The work compounds instead of dispersing.

A career should leave behind more than records. It should leave behind intelligence.

What Haarks is not

Precision matters more than ambition here.

  • Not an autonomous surgeon.
  • Not a diagnostic tool.
  • Not clinical decision support.
  • Not a replacement for surgical judgment.
  • Not a general chatbot pointed at medicine.

Haarks surfaces your own documented knowledge, with its sources, so you can decide. Not a Diagnostic Tool · Not Clinical Decision Support.

The future of surgery will not belong to those with the most information. It will belong to those who can remember it, connect it, and understand it.

Haarks is being built so that a career in surgery compounds, so that what you have already learned keeps working for you.
