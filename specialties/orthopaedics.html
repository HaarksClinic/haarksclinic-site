What Haarks means for orthopaedics

Over a career you accumulate a precise record of how you operate. Haarks turns that into a private, queryable layer, built only from what you document.

Whether you work in arthroplasty, trauma, spine, or sports, the value is the same: when you face a case that resembles one you have done before, you can ask what you actually did last time, and see the cases and protocols behind the answer.

Questions an orthopaedic surgeon might ask

Each retrieves from your own documented records. None asks Haarks to decide treatment.

  • "What did I do in my last four valgus TKRs?"
  • "Which implants did I previously use for revision hip arthroplasty?"
  • "Show me my previous distal femur fractures and the constructs I used."
  • "What complications did I document after my recent ACL reconstructions?"
  • "Which protocol did I follow for periprosthetic joint infection two-stage revision?"
  • "Show me my previous lumbar fusion cases and my documented levels."

Where the information comes from

  • Your logged cases — approach, implant, construct, complications, and follow-up you recorded.
  • Your Knowledge Vault — protocols, techniques, and reference documents you connect from your own storage.
  • Answers show the cases and documents they draw on, so you can verify the source.

Limitations

Haarks reflects only what you have documented. It does not diagnose, does not recommend implants or procedures, and is not a clinical decision-support system or medical device. Answers are a starting point for your own judgment, never a substitute for it. All clinical decisions remain the responsibility of the treating surgeon.

Common questions

Does Haarks recommend orthopaedic treatment?

No. Haarks retrieves and organises what you have personally documented. It does not recommend treatment, diagnose, or make clinical decisions.

Do I need to structure my cases a certain way?

You log cases by voice or text after a procedure. Haarks structures and connects them so they can be retrieved later; the more you document, the more your AI can reference.

Is my data shared with other surgeons?

No. Haarks uses per-surgeon isolation. Your knowledge is private to your account and is not used to train shared models. See the Privacy Policy.
