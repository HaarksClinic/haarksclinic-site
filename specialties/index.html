Specialties

Private AI, built around your specialty.

Haarks is your private AI for surgery. It answers from your own cases and protocols, with sources. The way it works is the same for every surgeon: it retrieves from the knowledge you have documented. What changes between specialties is the terminology, the workflow, and the questions you ask.
