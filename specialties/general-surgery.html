General Surgery

Your private AI for general surgery.

General surgery spans a wide range of procedures and a high case volume. That makes personal recall harder, and more valuable. Haarks retrieves from your own cases, operative notes, and protocols, with sources, so your own experience is the first thing you can search.

What Haarks means for general surgery

Across laparoscopic, emergency, and elective work, you build patterns in how you handle difficult anatomy, mesh, anastomoses, and follow-up. Haarks makes those patterns, as you recorded them, retrievable.

When a case echoes one you have managed before, you can ask what you actually did and see the cases and documents behind the answer, rather than relying on memory alone.

Questions a general surgeon might ask

Each retrieves from your own documented records. None asks Haarks to decide treatment.

  • "Show me my previous laparoscopic cholecystectomies where I noted a difficult Calot's triangle."
  • "Which mesh did I use in my last open inguinal hernia repairs?"
  • "What complications did I document after my recent colorectal anastomoses?"
  • "Show me my previous emergency laparotomies for perforation."
  • "Which protocol did I follow for enhanced recovery after major resection?"
  • "What did I do in my last appendicectomies that converted to open?"

Where the information comes from

  • Your logged cases — findings, technique, materials, complications, and follow-up you recorded.
  • Your Knowledge Vault — protocols, guidelines, and reference documents you connect from your own storage.
  • Answers show the cases and documents they draw on, so you can verify the source.

Limitations

Haarks reflects only what you have documented. It does not diagnose, does not recommend procedures, and is not a clinical decision-support system or medical device. Answers support your own judgment, never replace it. All clinical decisions remain the responsibility of the treating surgeon.

Common questions

Does Haarks decide how to operate?

No. Haarks retrieves what you have documented across your own cases and protocols. It does not diagnose or make clinical decisions.

How do cases get into Haarks?

You log them by voice or text after a procedure. Haarks structures and connects them so they are retrievable later.

Is my data private?

Yes. Haarks uses per-surgeon isolation and does not use your knowledge to train shared models. See the Privacy Policy.
